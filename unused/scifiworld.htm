Since watching Project Hail Mary I've quite liked a minimalist approach to space worldbuilding :3

Technology

Early-stage, grungy space-faring sci-fi setting — no crazy advanced technologies like teleportation or forcefields, or cool, clean, aesthetic technologies like holograms or FTL. Long-haul interplanetary space freighters with thousands of crew travelling are the primary means of travelling through deep space.

Solar system

Mercury is being mined out for iron and stuff.

The moon has been firmly established as a space port for interplanetary travel.

Mettra is a captured rogue planet with a highly eccentric orbit around the sun. It is the homeworld of the exceedingly hardy mettra, an alien species that take the appearance of bulky, blue-skinned, feminine humanoids with pointed ears, slightly exceeding humans in their average height. Mettra's gravity is significantly higher than Earth's, promoting higher muscle and bone density that allows mettra to achieve inhuman weight gain. Mettra are able to communicate telepathically with other mettra at any distance instantaneously (without first encoding the idea into language), an ability subject to continued scientific inquiry. Mettra are culturally distinct, with many formal behaviours humans would consider immodest.

Vordokt is a Venusian moon of Mettra with an incredibly dense atmosphere. It harbors non-sapient extremophiles.

Degbora is a habitable, Earth-like moon of Mettra, and the only non-Earth celestial body permanently inhabited by humans insofar.

Misc

Black Abyss Syndrome is a sudden feeling of distress experienced when staring into deep space for extended periods of time. The effect is reversed entirely immediately upon looking away from deep space, leaving no long-term effects. Nevertheless, spacefarers are discouraged from idling around windows when not orbiting a planet.

In extreme cases performed in controlled settings, individuals experiencing Black Abyss Syndrome reported hallucinations of ghost-like humanoid entities that have been described as 'angelic' and 'motherly.' Observing these entities induced such a strong sense of comfort during testing that shortly after participants' symptoms ceased entirely, even during follow-up exposure.

Of note is that mettra have never reported seeing angels, despite also experiencing Black Abyss Syndrome.
