Cultists are people who have been exhiled for attempting to commune with the 'old gods,' with the goal of getting the old gods to perform magic through them (preferrably both controllably and permanently, but cultists don't really care as long as something bad happens). No one actually knows what the old gods are (if there are multiple or just one, if they're simply an unconscious mechanism of the universe, or even what is necessary to do to successfully commune), but whatever they are, cultists have roused disaster by doing random things deep in the forest until something happens.

Writing snippets

I had heard this beastly shriek and doubled back across the small trek I had made; when I reached a wall I turned around and it was this sickly, toothy monster on all fours skittering my way, so I without aiming fired a shot from my rifle in its direction and it disappeared back into the fog...

The cultists worked under cover of darkness, gathering branches and stones from the forest to create an effigy to what they believed was a very, very powerful god. Similar pursuits had worked in the past, but inconsistently, and they recorded as much as they could of what they had done, the position of the stars, the fullness of their stomachs, anything; they worked obsessively to find the common factor.

The earliest records they have place the first experiments decades prior; before that, who knows. They refused to believe that it — any of it — was random; what would it say about the world that something so powerful was purely up to chance?

They burned their work before sunrise to cover their tracks.

An altar carved into the cliff-face obscured by the forest surrounding. The only indication towards its existence was the soft orange glow of the candles within, and more faintly, the blue glow of apparently nothing at all.
